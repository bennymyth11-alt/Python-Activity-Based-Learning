##  Overview of Selected Case Studies

1. **Case Study #8 – Manufacturing Cost and Profitability Analysis:**  
   Calculates material costs, labour charges, wastage, machine costs, and selling prices, and determines whether production meets a specified profit target.
2. **Case Study #12 – Attendance Recovery Computation:**  
   Calculates current attendance percentages and determines the minimum consecutive classes required to reach an eligibility threshold.
3. **Case Study #18 – Fuel Consumption and Route Comparison:**  
   Calculates fuel requirements and journey costs for several vehicles using distance, mileage, fuel prices, and reserve requirements to identify the lowest-cost feasible option.
4. **Case Study #33 – Campus Registration Identifier Analyzer:**  
   Examines student identification strings, validates their structure, extracts specified components, and classifies records according to branch and admission year codes.
5. **Case Study #39 – Multi-Stage Examination Eligibility System:**  
   Determines eligibility for a final examination using attendance, laboratory completion, internal assessment, and fee-clearance conditions, reporting every unmet requirement.
6. **Case Study #45 – Weekly Electricity Consumption Matrix:**  
   Processes appliance-wise electricity readings across seven days using nested lists, calculates daily and appliance totals, and identifies consumption exceeding specified limits.
7. **Case Study #49 – Tuple-Based Product Catalogue Audit:**  
   Stores product identifiers, prices, and stock quantities in tuples; detects invalid records, calculates inventory values, and identifies items requiring replenishment.
8. **Case Study #51 – Bus Passenger Occupancy Simulation:**  
   Processes boarding and alighting counts across multiple stops, calculates passenger counts at each stop, and flags any capacity violations.
9. **Case Study #53 – Food Waste Monitoring System:**  
   Compares prepared meals, meals served, and leftovers across several days; calculates wastage percentages and identifies days exceeding acceptable limits.
10. **Case Study #59 – Campus Transport Allocation:**  
    Determines the number of buses needed for several routes, accounts for bus capacities and passenger demand, and identifies unserved passengers.
